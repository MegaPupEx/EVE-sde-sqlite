## The headline finding

**Sim-only jumped 69% → 81% between generations because v1.5 moved the
mechanics into the engine.** In generation 1 the control had to *remember*
stacking exemptions and NPC profiles and got them wrong; now `set_env`,
`set_booster`, `set_projected` and `graph` compute them, and the tool
surface's own discipline (problems lists, unit keys, assumption strings)
carries the rest. The skill's remaining edge concentrates exactly where
it should: **discipline** (naming presets, builds, assumptions,
profiles), **the unmodeled** (G8: 1.5 vs 4 — the whole gap is one named
gap), and **the engine/game-knowledge boundary** (G6: prerequisites).
Growing the engine shrinks the docs' burden; the docs' job is what the
engine cannot say.
