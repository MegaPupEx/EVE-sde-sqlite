## What it means

1. **Seen-class fixes transferred; the underlying habit didn't.** Gen 6's
   named offenders stayed fixed, but on unseen mechanics sonnet-medium
   reverts to "this is general knowledge, answer directly" — q8's opener
   says it almost verbatim. The router's never-assert bullet was loaded
   (the SQL-only subjects clearly followed the data-discipline half) yet
   the *engine* half fired rarely.
2. **SQL is the comfort zone, the engine is the gap.** Six subjects ran
   SQL; only three touched the engine. Raw-attribute reading produced the
   q3 miss (base vs skill-modified — the same class as gen-6's storm
   resonance miss, one level milder) while the two engine-driven answers
   (q2, q6) were the cleanest of the run. The failure mode isn't laziness,
   it's stopping at layer 1 when the question needs layer 2.
3. **Fix applied, wording now mechanical:** the router bullet gains a
   self-audit rule — a mechanics answer with zero engine/SDE calls this
   turn is unverified by definition; make the call or label the claims.
   That converts compliance from judgment to arithmetic the subject can
   check against its own mandatory calls footer. Unmeasured as of this
   writing; the next held-out set (fresh classes again — these ten are
   now burned) is the test.
4. **Key-discipline scoreboard for this generation:** subjects corrected
   my keys zero times. The owner's review corrected one before launch
   (Q8 variants), and the derivation process itself caught two engine
   bugs and one of my own example errors (Large rig on a Drake). Deriving
   keys live against the tools, then having a human read them, is the
   protocol that finally beat the subjects to every error.

## Residual risk statement (product config, honest)

At sonnet-medium with the current docs: fit math, panels, and anything a
subject decides to compute are reliable; **unprompted mechanics questions
on classes the docs don't name carry a ~20% confident-wrong rate and
~40% imprecision rate** until the zero-call audit line proves out. The
mitigation that needs no model change: the two validation gaps this
round found are fixed, so the engine itself now refuses two of the ten
folklore traps (WCS count, rig size) the moment a subject builds the fit
— every validator fix converts a knowledge question into a tool answer.
